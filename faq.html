Account & Registration

Creating an account is easy! Just click the "Sign Up" button on the top right of any page. You'll need to provide:

  • Your full name
  • University email address
  • Phone number
  • A secure password
  • Your university and student ID

Once you submit, you'll receive a verification email to confirm your account.

Yes! We require a valid university email address (.edu.ng or your university's domain) to verify that you're a student. This helps us maintain a trusted community of verified students only.

If your university isn't on our list yet, you can join the waitlist and we'll notify you when your university goes live.

Student verification is our way of ensuring every user is a real student. The process includes:

  • Email Verification: We send a confirmation link to your university email
  • Student ID: You'll be asked to upload a photo of your student ID
  • Identity Check: Our team reviews your information within 24 hours

Once verified, you'll receive a "Verified" badge on your profile and unlock all features.

No worries! Click the "Forgot Password" link on the login page. We'll send you a password reset link to your registered email address. Follow the instructions to create a new password.

If you don't receive the email, check your spam folder or contact our support team.

Yes, you can update your email address in your account settings. However, you'll need to verify the new email address before it becomes active.

Note that you must use a valid university email address to remain verified.

Buying on Relov

You can find items in several ways:

  • Browse: Explore categories like Furniture, Electronics, Books, Fashion, and more
  • Search: Use the search bar to find specific items
  • Filters: Narrow down results by price, condition, university, and category
  • Wishlist: Save items you're interested in for later

Each product listing has a "Chat with Seller" button. Click it to start a conversation. You can:

  • Ask questions about the item
  • Negotiate the price
  • Arrange a meet-up time and location

All communication happens within Relov's secure messaging system. Your personal contact information stays private.

On the product detail page, click the "Make Offer" button. You'll be able to:

  • Propose your offer price
  • Add a message to the seller explaining your offer
  • Submit your offer for the seller to review

The seller can accept, decline, or counter your offer.

We recommend meeting on campus in a safe, public location. Popular meet-up spots include:

  • Student Center
  • University Library
  • Main Campus Entrance
  • Designated pickup points

Always bring a friend if possible and choose a well-lit, populated area during daylight hours.

Returns are handled between the buyer and seller. We recommend:

  • Inspecting the item carefully before purchase
  • Asking the seller about their return policy
  • Communicating any issues directly with the seller

If you can't resolve an issue, contact our support team for assistance.

Selling on Relov

Listing an item is easy and free! Just click the "Sell" button and follow the steps:

  1. Add photos of your item (up to 5)
  2. Write a clear title and description
  3. Select the category and condition
  4. Set your price in Naira (₦)
  5. Choose your meet-up location
  6. Review and publish your listing

Your listing will be live immediately and visible to all students at your university.

Nothing! Relov is completely free for students. We don't charge:

  • No listing fees
  • No commission on sales
  • No hidden charges

We believe in helping students save and earn without taking a cut.

You can sell almost anything! Popular categories include:

  • Furniture: Chairs, tables, beds, bookshelves
  • Electronics: Laptops, phones, headphones, gaming consoles
  • Books: Textbooks, novels, study guides
  • Fashion: Clothing, shoes, accessories
  • Kitchen: Utensils, small appliances, dinnerware
  • Services: Tutoring, photography, laundry, hair styling

Please don't list prohibited items like weapons, illegal substances, or counterfeit goods.

Don't worry! Here are some tips to help your item sell:

  • Great Photos: Take clear, well-lit photos from multiple angles
  • Accurate Description: Be honest about the condition and include key details
  • Competitive Price: Research similar items and price competitively
  • Quick Response: Reply to messages promptly

You can also edit your listing to adjust the price or description at any time.

Yes! You can manage your listings from your dashboard:

  • Edit: Update price, description, photos, or availability
  • Delete: Remove the listing if the item is no longer available
  • Mark as Sold: Let buyers know the item has been sold

Safety & Trust

Yes! Relov is designed with safety as our top priority. Here's what we do:

  • Student Verification: Every user must verify their student identity
  • Secure Messaging: All communication happens within our platform
  • Campus Meetups: We encourage public, on-campus meetings
  • Rating System: See reviews from other students
  • 24/7 Monitoring: Our team monitors for suspicious activity
  • Report Feature: Easily report any issues

If you see a listing that seems suspicious, you can report it by:

  1. Clicking the "Report" button on the product detail page
  2. Selecting the reason for reporting
  3. Adding any additional details

Our team will review the report within 24 hours and take appropriate action.

Follow these safety tips for a secure meet-up:

  • Meet in Public: Choose a well-lit, populated location on campus
  • Bring a Friend: Don't go alone if possible
  • Daylight Hours: Schedule meetups during the day
  • Trust Your Instincts: If something feels off, cancel the meetup
  • Inspect the Item: Check the item before paying
  • Use Cash or Transfer: Bring exact change or use bank transfer

If you have a dispute:

  1. Try to resolve it directly with the other party
  2. If that doesn't work, contact our support team
  3. We'll review the situation and help mediate

We take all disputes seriously and will work to find a fair resolution.
